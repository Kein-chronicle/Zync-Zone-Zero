## Extracted ZZZ Player Character Lessons

ZZZ playable characters are readable because they combine:

- Strong silhouette first.
- One obvious combat prop or weapon.
- Urban fashion mixed with combat practicality.
- High-contrast accessories.
- Faction/personality coded through outfit details.
- Animation identity that matches combat role.

For Zync Zone Zero, use those lessons but make original characters:

- No copied costumes.
- No copied faction motifs.
- No copied weapons with the same shape language.
- No copied hair/eye/accessory combinations.
- No direct palette lifts.

## Player Character Art Direction

### Global Rule

Playable characters are cute anime-style female fighters, but must be combat-readable and adult-coded.

Rules:

- Avoid school-uniform defaulting.
- Avoid childlike proportions.
- Use adult-coded height/proportions even in pixel form.
- Outfit must explain combat role.
- Every character needs a distinct weapon/read silhouette.
- Front, rear, and three-quarter-back reads matter because the game view shows the player's back.

### Party Roles

#### Z-01: Balanced Lead

Design goal:

- Clean, readable, default heroine.
- Works as the tutorial baseline.
- Medium weapon, medium motion size.

Pixel silhouette:

- Shoulder-length or tied hair for readable head shape.
- Short jacket or cropped combat coat.
- One medium blade or baton-like weapon.
- Bright accent on weapon edge for attack timing.

Color rule:

- Neutral dark base.
- Cyan or white timing accent.
- Do not over-detail the torso; keep limbs readable.

#### Z-02: Heavy Breaker

Design goal:

- Slower, weightier, high Groggy pressure.
- Tag parry should feel heavy.

Pixel silhouette:

- Larger weapon or shield module.
- Wider stance.
- Heavier boots/gloves.
- Hair shape should not hide weapon read.

Color rule:

- Warm yellow/gold accent.
- Heavier dark body blocks.
- Impact frames can use square, chunky hit effects.

#### Z-03: Fast Counter

Design goal:

- Fast, agile, dodge/counter specialist.
- Tag-in should feel sharp.

Pixel silhouette:

- Slimmer stance.
- Long scarf, ribbon, cable, or afterimage trail.
- Dual short weapons or compact ranged weapon.
- Hair/accessory motion can show speed.

Color rule:

- Teal/green accent.
- More narrow bright streaks.
- Afterimage frames should be simple and readable.

## Enemy Art Direction

Enemies are grotesque alien/corrupted forms, not normal humans.

Core ideas to steal structurally from ZZZ Ethereals:

- Corrupted core inside the body.
- Crystalline armor or growths protecting the core.
- Body distortion from alien energy.
- Mutation from environment/object fragments.
- Mixture of biological and artificial shapes.
- Hostile, unreadable emotion.

Original Zync rules:

- Enemies are not called Ethereals.
- Avoid identical black-white-green ZZZ monster palettes.
- Avoid copying specific enemy silhouettes.
- Use our own cosmic rhythm/corruption concept.

### Enemy Visual Keywords

- Alien
- Grotesque
- Asymmetric
- Crystalline
- Hollow-bodied
- Too many joints
- Mask-like head
- Split torso
- Exposed core
- Floating fragments
- Corrupted machinery
- Organic cables
- Unnatural rhythm pulse

## Pixel Art Rules

### Resolution Targets

Prototype scale:

- Player character base sprite: 96px to 128px tall.
- Support character sprite: same asset scaled or drawn slightly smaller in scene.
- Boss sprite: 260px to 420px tall depending on encounter.
- Hit effects: 32px to 160px depending on impact.

Production target:

- Author sprites at 2x or 4x working resolution if needed.
- Export crisp pixel scale.
- No interpolation blur.

### Readability Rules

- Silhouette must read in one color.
- Weapon shape must read from behind.
- Active character must be visually stronger than supports.
- Yellow/red boss states must be readable without text.
- Do not rely on tiny facial details.
- Use hair/accessory motion for rhythm, not idle clutter.

### Palette Rules

Players:

- Shared dark neutral combat base.
- One role accent per character.
- Skin/hair colors can vary, but keep combat effects more saturated than outfit trim.

Bosses:

- Dark body mass.
- Distorted secondary color.
- Core color distinct from yellow/red attack warnings.
- Yellow reserved for parryable warning.
- Red reserved for unparryable warning.

### Animation Frame Rules

Minimum playable prototype:

- Idle: 4 frames
- Weak 1/2/3: 4 to 6 frames each
- Heavy 1/2: 6 to 8 frames each
- Dodge: 5 frames
- Tag-in: 5 frames
- Tag parry: 6 to 8 frames
- Support auto attack: 4 frames
- Hit reaction: 3 frames

Boss minimum:

- Idle: 4 frames
- Windup: 4 to 6 frames
- Impact: 2 to 3 frames
- Recovery: 4 frames
- Groggy: 4 frames
- Death/clear: 6 to 10 frames

## Reference Use Rules

Allowed:

- Use ZZZ references to study silhouette, role readability, animation exaggeration, urban fantasy styling, and monster body logic.
- Use multiple references per concept, then abstract into original rules.
- Use color warning logic only as a generic combat readability concept.

Not allowed:

- Directly copying a ZZZ character outfit.
- Tracing official art.
- Reusing ZZZ enemy silhouettes.
- Copying names, factions, weapon props, or exact palettes.
- Making a character recognizable as a ZZZ fan character.
