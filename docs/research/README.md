# Research

Research hub for Zync Zone Zero.

## Research Tracks

- Rhythm action combat references
- Input timing and judgment systems
- Beat-synced enemy AI patterns
- Steam PC market positioning
- Visual and audio direction references

## Initial Questions

- Is combat driven by strict beat timing, loose musical windows, or hybrid action priority?
- Does the player attack on beat, dodge on beat, parry on beat, or chain all actions to rhythm?
- Should the game feel closer to character action, roguelite arena combat, or stage-based rhythm combat?
- What makes the rhythm layer necessary instead of decorative?
