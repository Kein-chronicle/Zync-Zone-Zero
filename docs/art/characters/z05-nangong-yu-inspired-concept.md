# Z-05 Character Concept

## Reference Extraction

Reference target: Zenless Zone Zero, Nangong Yu / Korean name Namgung'u.

Reference facts used:

- female Thiren agent
- black shoulder-length hair
- red eyes
- Angels of Delusion idol and lead-dancer context
- Ether, Stun, Strike combat identity
- combat language around Downbeats, precise charging, Vibrato, and dance timing

Traits to reference:

- idol-dancer combat rhythm
- black bob hair and red-eye focal point
- cute but sharp performer energy
- ether-pink and violet stage-light accents
- stun-building heavy beats and precise timing

Traits not to copy:

- exact costume, faction marks, official symbols, hairstyle cut, facial design, weapon design, name, story role, or recognizable silhouette
- exact ZZZ UI marks, ability names as visual labels, or official color layout

Original direction:

Z-05 is an original rhythm-action idol striker. She fights with compact ether beat gauntlets and stage-light impact bursts, not with Nangong Yu's exact moves or costume. The design reads as a 2-head-tall chibi anime heroine with a black bob, red eyes, cropped performance jacket, short skirt armor, and magenta-violet beat hardware.

## Identity Lock

Character ID: Z-05
Role: rhythm idol stun striker
Combat identity: precise beat attacker who builds groggy through charged dance-step impacts
Body proportion: 2-head-tall chibi anime heroine, young adult-coded, compact idol-combat silhouette
Face: red eyes, visible eyelids, tiny confident mouth, sharp performer expression in concept/front references
Hair: black shoulder-length bob with blunt bangs, small side ribbon and short back flicks
Head silhouette: rounded bob mass with two small ribbon points, no animal ears
Outfit: cropped white-and-charcoal performance jacket, black inner top, compact magenta skirt armor, thigh-high socks, short boots, fingerless gloves
Weapon: pair of compact ether beat gauntlets with small floating ring emitters near the wrists
Weapon idle state: gauntlets always worn on both hands; ring emitters hover close to wrists
Weapon visibility rule: gauntlets and wrist emitters must remain visible in idle, breathing, recovery, guard, ready, walk, and tag frames
Palette: black hair, warm skin, charcoal, clean white, magenta, violet, red eye accents
Accent color: #ff5fd7
Silhouette: black bob head mass, cropped idol jacket, magenta skirt plate, wrist ring emitters
Do not change: black bob, red eyes, magenta-violet ether gauntlets, idol jacket silhouette, wrist emitters, upward combat direction
