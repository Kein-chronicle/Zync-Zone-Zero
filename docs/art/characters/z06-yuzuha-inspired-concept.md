# Z-06 Character Concept

## Reference Extraction

Reference target: Zenless Zone Zero, Ukinami Yuzuha / Korean name Yuzuha.

Reference facts used:

- Physical Support combat role
- Spook Shack association
- Sugar Points and Tanuki Cloak style mechanics
- playful trickster support energy
- tanuki helper motif
- defensive assist, parry, and follow-up support feel

Traits to reference:

- cute trickster support fighter
- candy/charm/tanuki-inspired rhythm feedback
- playful but combat-readable support identity
- warm orange, cream, teal, and candy-pink accent direction
- compact umbrella/charm weapon language

Traits not to copy:

- exact costume, faction marks, official symbols, story role, name, hairstyle, face, weapon design, mascot design, or recognizable silhouette
- exact ZZZ UI marks, ability names as visual labels, or official color layout

Original direction:

Z-06 is an original rhythm-action charm support striker. She uses a compact charm umbrella and small floating candy talismans to create defensive pulses, parry beats, and playful support hits. The design reads as a 2-head-tall chibi anime heroine with a warm bob, small ribboned hood cape, cream-and-orange street shrine outfit, teal charm tassels, and candy-pink timing sparks.

## Identity Lock

Character ID: Z-06
Role: trickster charm support
Combat identity: playful support striker who uses umbrella taps, charm pulses, and candy-like rhythm sparks to assist and build pressure
Body proportion: 2-head-tall chibi anime heroine, young adult-coded, compact support silhouette
Face: amber eyes, visible eyelids, tiny mischievous smile in concept/front references
Hair: warm chestnut bob with soft side locks and a small cream-orange ribbon
Head silhouette: rounded bob plus small hood-cape points; no animal ears copied from reference
Outfit: cropped cream hood-cape over orange-black combat kimono jacket, short pleated skirt armor, teal charm tassels, thigh-high socks, short boots, fingerless gloves
Weapon: compact charm umbrella with dangling teal paper talismans and candy-pink bead charms
Weapon idle state: umbrella always held in right hand, folded and lowered diagonally beside the body
Weapon visibility rule: umbrella and charm tassels must remain visible in idle, breathing, recovery, guard, ready, walk, tag, and ultimate frames
Palette: chestnut hair, warm skin, cream, burnt orange, charcoal black, teal charm accents, candy pink spark accents
Accent color: #ff9f43
Silhouette: round chestnut bob, small hood cape, folded umbrella, teal charm tassels, candy-pink support sparks
Do not change: chestnut bob, cream-orange support outfit, right-hand charm umbrella, teal tassels, candy-pink timing sparks, upward combat direction
