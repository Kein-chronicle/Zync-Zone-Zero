# Z-04 Character Concept

## Reference Extraction

Reference target: Zenless Zone Zero, Hoshimi Miyabi.

Traits to reference:

- disciplined frost swordswoman tone
- elegant samurai/iaido combat identity
- animal-ear silhouette as a strong head-shape read
- cool, restrained palette with bright icy attack accents
- composed elite-agent presence rather than cheerful brawler energy

Traits not to copy:

- exact costume, faction marks, hairstyle, face, weapon shape, color layout, name, or story identity
- recognizable Miyabi silhouette
- official symbols, UI marks, or ornament patterns

Original direction:

Z-04 is an original rhythm-action frost iaido heroine. She uses a short nodachi-style energy blade and a compact haori-jacket silhouette. Her head read comes from angular fox-ear hair accessories rather than copying Miyabi's exact ear shape. Her palette uses black graphite, cold white, glacial blue, and a small violet accent.
